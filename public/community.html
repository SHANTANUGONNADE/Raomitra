Is Cubbon Park safe for a sunrise walk?

Landing at 5 AM and want a quiet first morning. Any local tips on gates, coffee nearby, or areas to skip?

Priya Sharma: Yes — enter from the MG Road side after 6 AM. Skip the dark corners near the library until full daylight. Third Wave Coffee is a 6-minute walk.
